Grade 1 – Place Value

Place value tells us what each digit in a number means.

12 = 1 ten and 2 ones
35 = 3 tens and 5 ones
48 = 4 tens and 8 ones

Tens are groups of 10. Ones are single units.

⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ = 10 (one ten)
⭐⭐⭐ = 3 ones

Place value helps you understand bigger numbers and do harder math later.
