Grade 1 – Shapes

Shapes are all around us! Here are the basic shapes you learn in Grade 1:

● Circle – round, no corners
■ Square – 4 equal sides
▲ Triangle – 3 sides
⬛ Rectangle – 4 sides (2 long, 2 short)
⬤ Oval – like a stretched circle

Shapes help us understand the world. You see them in signs, toys, buildings, and everywhere!
